Sunday mornings are just built different when you're out with the energetic crew from WNC!

There’s something magical about an early morning start with people who share your passion for the outdoors.

This Sunday, the WNC team headed out for a birding expedition that turned into an unforgettable morning. We were greeted by the vibrant chatter and feeding antics of Plum-headed Parakeets, a striking pair of Pied Kingfishers, busy starlings,group of small minivets and plenty of other feathered friends.

To top it all off, we explored a picturesque heritage train route, capped off the walk with great snacks by the kund, and shared plenty of laughs. Moments like these remind us why we love exploring the outdoors together.
This morning was absolute perfection..
Team WNC
